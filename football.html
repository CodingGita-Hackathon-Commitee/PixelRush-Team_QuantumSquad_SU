📄 About the Event

The Campus Football League (CFL 2026) reaches its grand finale! Join 1,200+ passionate students and alumni under the stadium floodlights as the indomitable Faculty of Engineering Titans clash against the reigning champions, Faculty of Management Dynamos.

Expect intense tactical football, roaring student sections, halftime performances by the varsity dance troupe, and food stalls from popular campus eateries. Every ticket guarantees entry to East Bleachers and a chance to win exclusive matchday merchandise.
