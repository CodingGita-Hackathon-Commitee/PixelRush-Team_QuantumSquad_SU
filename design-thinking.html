📄 About the Event

The Design Thinking Workshop 2026 is an intensive 4-hour hands-on sprint dedicated to human-centered product development, UX research heuristics, and interactive digital prototyping. Students and campus innovators collaborate in cross-functional pods to transform ambiguity into tangible products using modern design methodologies.

Whether you are exploring UI/UX for the first time or sharpening your portfolio for upcoming tech recruiting sprints, this masterclass equips you with real-world case interview techniques, user empathy interviews, rapid paper sketching, and Figma component libraries.
