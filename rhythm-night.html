📄 About the Event

Rhythm Night 2026 is the premier annual university music festival celebrating collegiate talent, sonic experimentation, and campus unity under the starlit sky. Featuring high-voltage indie rock bands, intimate acoustic sets, and an electronic dance set with laser effects, this live showcase brings students together for an unforgettable night of high-fidelity music, artisanal refreshments, and vibrant energy.
